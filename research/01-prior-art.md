# FRONTIER Research: Prior Art and Originality

- **VERIFIED:** NEP-245 is a final multi-token standard for fungible, semi-fungible, non-fungible, and other token types in one contract. It supports batch transfer and standardized mint, burn, and transfer events.
- **VERIFIED:** NEP-171 is the standard NFT interface and its event form is appropriate for port ownership records.
- **INFERRED:** FRONTIER's strongest distinction is not simply tokenizing ships; it is coupling port NFT ownership to market configuration and treasury state, then making trade routes and hidden raids determine traffic.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** A visible faucet/sink dashboard can make the game's no-patch economy legible to judges and players.

## Difference in two sentences

Existing on-chain games demonstrate persistent state, hidden actions, or resource economies independently. FRONTIER combines a player-owned port market, commit-reveal convoy risk, and an explicit economy balance panel in a short arbitrage loop on NEAR.
