# FRONTIER demo narration

Voice: Microsoft Zira Desktop, generated locally. Synthetic narration, not a human voice clone. Footage: real public app and testnet transactions; waits shortened.

## FRONTIER / REDESIGNED LIVE ROUND

A fresh captain trades, hires an escort, crosses a live ambush window with every unit of cargo intact, and sells at the destination. Every order shown here is a real NEAR testnet transaction.

## 01 / CONNECT AND REGISTER

For this recording, an automated wallet approves only the game contract methods. The full-access testnet credential never enters the page. This fresh captain then registers through the redesigned interface.

## 02 / BUY CARGO AND BUILD A SLOOP

The captain spends 400 CRED on Blockspace and builds a defence-one sloop. The market quote and resulting inventory both come from the deployed contract.

## 03 / HIRE A DEFENCE-THREE ESCORT

A second fresh captain has built a galleon with exactly three defence. The convoy looks up that live offer and hires it through the interface before departure.

## 04 / REVEALED AMBUSH AND SEALED DEPARTURE

A scripted rival has exactly two idle defence, above the sloop alone but below the escorted convoy's total defence of four. Its committed and revealed ambush window is established first; then the captain loads every unit and departs inside that exact window.

## 05 / ARRIVE WITH FULL CARGO

The contract resolves the voyage after the route distance. The proof compares the loaded ship cargo with the captain's credited inventory, alongside the exact rival power and window, rather than inferring protection from an event flag.

## 06 / SELL AT THE DESTINATION

At Solana Reef, the captain sells every surviving unit through the live market. The final state confirms the Blockspace inventory was sold after arriving intact. The sale price is whatever the live market pays; this voyage is not a profit claim.

## 07 / SET AND SPRING AN AMBUSH

Now the captain turns raider. The idle sloop sets an ambush on the Bitcoin Isle to Solana Reef route with a window opening in 10 blocks, then springs it through the interface.

## 08 / RECEIPTS AND INDEPENDENT PROOF

The capture record links every transaction and records the escort's defence, rival's complete fresh fleet, ambush bounds, exact departure block, and equal loaded and arrived cargo. This is live testnet evidence, not a simulated outcome.
