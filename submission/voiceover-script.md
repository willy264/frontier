# FRONTIER demo narration

Voice: Microsoft Hazel Desktop, generated locally. Synthetic narration, not a human voice clone. Footage: real public app and testnet transactions; waits shortened.

## FRONTIER / REAL TESTNET GAMEPLAY

Trade goods. Choose your route. Risk an ambush. This is Frontier, a strategy and trading game on NEAR testnet. These are real transactions in the deployed app.

## 01 / CONNECT AND REGISTER

For this recording, an automated testnet wallet approves a game-only key. The key is restricted to this contract. Registering gives our new captain one thousand CRED, the non-redeemable game currency.

## 02 / BUY CARGO AND BUILD A SHIP

The port market reads its reserves and owner fee from the contract. We spend three hundred CRED on Hashpower, then build a sloop. Every purchase changes the market and our on-chain inventory.

## 03 / SEAL THE DESTINATION

We load 41 units of Hashpower and seal the destination with a browser-generated salt. A scripted rival has placed a real ambush on this route. The backup download preserves the secret needed for arrival.

## 04 / REVEAL AND RESOLVE

Once the travel time passes, we reveal the destination. The contract validates the route and resolves the ambush. The result feed reports the raid and cargo loss. These outcomes come from contract events.

## 05 / SELL THE SURVIVING GOODS

Back at the market panel, we sell the surviving Hashpower at the current quote. The CRED balance changes again. This run shows the cost of fees and a raid; it does not promise a trading profit.

## 06 / COMMIT AND REVEAL AN AMBUSH

A player can also become a raider. We choose another route, stake ten CRED, save the salt, and reveal the ambush. The browser exposes the reveal control and keeps a downloadable backup.

## 07 / INSPECT THE ON-CHAIN ECONOMY

The economy panel reads live supply, faucet, and spoilage totals. Ports collect trading fees for their owners. This is a testnet MVP; cross-chain Intents settlement and production season rollover are future work.
