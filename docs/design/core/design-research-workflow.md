# Design research workflow

Time box research to 45-90 minutes. The output is `docs/design/reference-sheet.md`.

## Sources

Use live sites, Awwwards, Lapa Ninja, Land-book, Godly, details.so, Mobbin, and the project's own references for patterns. Treat shots as concepts, not implementation assets.

## Scoring

Score each reference 0-2 for hierarchy, restrained palette, type, spacing, real content, and accessibility. Keep references scoring at least 8/12. Reject purple blobs, glass everywhere, 3D emoji, unreadable text, and fake charts.

## Record decisions

For each reference record what to take and what not to take:

| Reference | Take | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| Name and link | Pattern or behavior | Token, blueprint, or component decision |

Take patterns, never assets, copy, logos, illustrations, or code. Compare the first build at 1440px and 375px, list three differences, and fix the highest-impact mistakes.
