# Method for a new niche

1. Identify risk involving money, identity, health, children, or legal claims.
2. Study three live sites and at least eight references. Record what to take and what to reject.
3. Choose a base pack: gaming, fintech, professional, or playful.
4. Override only accent, neutral temperature, fonts, radius, imagery, motion, required sections, and voice.
5. Add a niche PRD, design system, and copy file before writing UI.
6. Run the core QA checklist unchanged.

Take patterns, never assets, copy, logos, illustrations, or code.
