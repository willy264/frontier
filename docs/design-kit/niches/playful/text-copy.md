# Playful: copy overrides

Applies on top of `core/text-copy.md`.

## 1. Register

Warm, second person, sensory and local. Encouraging errors. Never a disappointed teacher. [3P: Duolingo] [OWN: QuickBite]

| Slot | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Concrete promise + one rotating word if it adds information | "Real food, delivered [Fresh./Fast./Local.]" [OWN] |
| Section H2 | Question or two-beat line, each beat concrete | "What are you craving?", "Good questions. Clear answers." [OWN] |
| Subtitle | Sensory, local | "From smoky jollof to late-night small chops, pick a category and dig in." [OWN] |
| Microcopy | Honest and local | "We started in Ile-Ife and we're just getting going. Don't see your city yet? It's probably next on the map." [OWN] |
| Steps | Verb-first, specific to the product | "Pick your meal", "Pay with Paystack", "Track to your door" [OWN] |
| CTAs | Sentence case, verb-first | "Order now", "Explore restaurants", "Get launch updates", "Become a partner", "Ride with us" [OWN] |
| CTA verbs | Start, Continue, Try, Save | [3P: Duolingo] |
| Avoid | "Learn more", "Click here" | [3P: Duolingo] |

QuickBite's "Find, Order & Enjoy" is a reflexive triad in title case. Prefer one concrete line in new projects.

## 2. Errors and feedback

| Situation | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong answer | "Wrong." | "Hmm, not quite." [3P: Duolingo] |
| Address not served | "Service unavailable in your area." | "We don't deliver to <area> yet. Get a message when we do." [SYN] |
| Payment failed | "Payment error." | "Your card wasn't charged. Try again or pay another way." [SYN] |
| Empty cart | "Cart is empty." | "Your cart's empty. Find something tasty." [SYN] |
| Success | "Order submitted." | "Order placed. <Kitchen> is on it." [SYN] |

Warm does not mean vague: every error still says what happened and what to do. No "Oops!" plus "sorry".

## 3. Celebration

- Celebrate progress briefly ("3-day streak!"); one exclamation mark at most.
- Rewards match a real event. No celebration for routine actions like saving settings.

## 4. Watch-outs

- Puns once per page at most.
- Emoji in UI copy only if the brand uses them; never in headings. [DOC: humanizer]
- Typewriter or rotating words must each add meaning; the static text must read fine on its own (screen readers and reduced motion).
